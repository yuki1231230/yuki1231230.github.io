What it does

Satoshi Auto Post uploads short Japanese vocabulary lessons for learners around the world. The videos are created by us and are posted only to our own accounts, three times a day.

Satoshi Auto Post は、外国の人向けに作った日本語レッスン動画を、自分たちのアカウントに1日3回、自動で投稿するための個人用ツールです。

Accounts it posts to

  • YouTube: @Satoshi_From_Japan
  • Instagram: @satoshi___japan2, @satoshijapan2, @kirara___japan

How it works

  • Videos are stored in a private repository owned by us.
  • On a schedule, the tool picks one video, writes a title and description in English and Japanese, and uploads it using the official platform APIs.
  • It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account.
