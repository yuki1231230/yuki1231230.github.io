Sign in with TikTok once to let Satoshi Auto Post publish your own Japanese-lesson videos to your account on a schedule.

What the app will be allowed to do

  • user.info.basic: read your display name and open id to confirm the correct account
  • video.upload and video.publish: upload one of your videos and post it to your profile

How posting works

  1. Before each post, the app checks your account's allowed privacy options and maximum video length.
  2. It uploads one video with a caption in English and Japanese.
  3. It publishes the video to your profile with the privacy level you chose in the settings.
